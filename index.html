Cardcase

Scan business cards. Keep them on your phone.

Cardcase is an Android app that turns business cards into a searchable contact list. Point the camera at a card and it reads the name, company, phone, email and website for you.

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no server.
  • The app has no internet permission: your cards stay on your phone.
  • Text is read on the phone itself; photos are never uploaded.
  • Export everything whenever you like: cards, photos, a spreadsheet, and a page you can open on your PC.

What it does

  • Scan the front and back of a card; fix anything the scanner misread.
  • Sort cards into categories and search by anything printed on them.
  • Call, text, email or visit a website in one tap.
  • Share a card as a text message or a contact file.
