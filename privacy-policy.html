Cardcase Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Cardcase keeps your cards on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server, and the app does not have permission to use the internet. Your cards leave your phone only when you share or export them yourself, or through Android's own backup if you have it turned on.

Who we are

Cardcase is an Android app made by Matt Postiff ("we"). Questions about this policy: cardcase-android@proton.me.

Information Cardcase stores

Cardcase stores what you put into it, on your phone only:

  • Card details you scan or type: names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, categories and notes.
  • Photos of the cards you scan or choose from your gallery, and the text read from them.
  • A few app settings, such as when you last exported your cards.

We do not collect, receive or store any of this information. There is no Cardcase account and no Cardcase server.

How Cardcase uses your phone's features

  • Camera: to photograph cards. Photos are saved inside the app.
  • Photo gallery: only the photo you pick is given to the app.
  • Text recognition: text is read from card photos on the phone itself, using Google ML Kit built into the app. Photos are not sent anywhere for this.
  • Card scanner: the camera scanner, which finds and straightens the card, is provided by Google Play services on your phone. Cardcase receives the finished photo. Google Play services is part of Android and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • No internet access: the app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

When your information leaves your phone

Only in these cases, each started by you:

  • Sharing a card: when you tap Share, the card goes to the app and person you choose (for example a text message or email), with the details you select. What happens next is up to that app and person.
  • Exporting: Export saves your cards and photos to a folder or .zip file you choose. If that place is synced to a cloud service, that service's terms apply.
  • Android backup: if Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy Cardcase's card database (the text of your cards, not the photos) to your Google account, and copies everything, photos included, when you transfer to a new phone. This is done by Android and Google, not by Cardcase, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings.

We do not sell, share or receive your information. Cardcase contains no advertising or tracking code.

Other people's information

The cards you store hold other people's contact details. Please keep them only as you would any contact list, and share them only as those people would expect.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your cards stay on your phone until you delete them. Deleting a card in Cardcase removes it; uninstalling Cardcase removes all of its cards and photos from the phone. Exports you saved and Android backups are not removed by uninstalling; delete those yourself if you wish.

Children

Cardcase is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
